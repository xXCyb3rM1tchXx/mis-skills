## Benchmark contract
The validated AVA artifact is a historical benchmark for DEPTH only. The user never needs to attach AVA.

A. Executive Brief: why prospecting/prioritized; stakeholders; entry point + authority limit; signals; opportunity to explore; critical gaps; red flags; initial strategy; Next Best Action.
B. Profile: structure, units/divisions, geography, relevant operations, scale/workforce and limits.
C. Stakeholders: ALL relevant/validated stakeholders with role, validation, contact, potential role, relevance, authority limits and useful professional context already available.
D. Account Intelligence: ALL material Stage 8 signals with Evidence → demonstrates → does NOT demonstrate → inference → confidence → Discovery implication.
E. RH/benefits/programs: concrete account-specific items with classification and limits.
F. Fit: each relevant category classified with rationale.
G. Gap Map: status + unknown + why it matters for headcount/exposed, eligible population, dependents, shifts, benefits, EAP/telemed, medical/dental, provider/broker, utilization, budget, Benefits/C&B/Wellness owner, buying committee, procurement, autonomy, process, timing/renewal, pains, turnover/absenteeism, priorities/KPIs and competition. If not applicable, mark NO APLICA with reason.
H. Strategy: reason to reach out, why entry point, positioning, what not to assume, CTA, channel and limits.
I. Follow-up: cadence, purpose, channel, angle change, multithreading, pause and recycle.
J. No-response tree: silence, redirection, wrong contact, interest/no timing and reasonable attempt exhaustion.
K. Discovery: exactly 5 questions, each with Why / What to discover / What to listen for, linked to real gaps/hypotheses.
L. Objections: each with What it may mean / What to ask / What NOT to assume / Reasonable move. When relevant include existing provider, corporate decision, budget/timing, “send me information”, not a priority and similar benefits.
M. Signals to advance: what evidence justifies deeper Discovery, another stakeholder, data request, proposal, nurture or re-evaluate fit.
N. Next Best Actions: concrete, sequenced and account-specific.
O. Unknown Register: State / Why it matters / When / Who could confirm.
P. Provenance: enough identification to audit critical claims.
Q. Sales Handoff Final: concise execution summary, never a replacement for the Book.

# Failure Recovery, Privacy and Platform Adapters

## Failure recovery
Context degradation → require explicit artifacts; no false PASS from compressed memory.
Native Deep Research exhausted at Stage 8 → Gemini fallback.
Gemini dossier structurally incomplete → BLOCKED_BY_INCOMPLETE_EXTERNAL_RESEARCH with exact missing items.
Paid provider plan block → do not treat as “not found.”
Credit exhaustion → do not auto-switch vendor; use validated no-cost fallback or BLOCKED_BY_CREDITS.
Deep Research accidentally invoked in Stages 9–12 → stop and rerun as normal transformation.
Stage 11 low density → BENCHMARK_PARITY_TEST FAIL; expand only from existing artifacts.
Stage 11 inconsistent visual design → VISUAL_SYSTEM_GATE FAIL; restyle without changing content.
Stage 12 ambiguous Drive → ask only for DRIVE DESTINO.

## Privacy
Operational tasks may use full authorized business contact data when necessary.
Skill docs/examples/benchmarks must anonymize surnames, emails, phones, profile URLs and provider job IDs.
Historical accounts/people in documentation are NEVER active campaign state.
Gemini fallback should minimize PII: names/roles may be necessary; email/phone normally are not.

## Platform adapter model
Host-neutral business logic.
- Account discovery: Apollo organization/account search preferred.
- People discovery: public web research is validated fallback when Apollo People is plan-blocked.
- Identity validation: FullEnrich free preview/search when available.
- Work email: FullEnrich only under explicit spend approval.
- Public research: host web/browser.
- Deep Research: native only Stage 8; Gemini fallback if unavailable.
- CRM: manual GHL payload.
- Archive/tracking: Drive/Sheets when authorized, otherwise manual.

ChatGPT Work: use installed plugins/connectors. Tool visibility ≠ account connected ≠ plan authorized ≠ cost confirmed.
Claude Cowork: use available connectors/MCPs with same gates. If a connector is absent, apply the current-stage PRECHECK and documented fallback; defer later-stage gaps.
